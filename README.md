# XYZP Hospitality H1 Revenue Performance Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents a financial and revenue performance analysis of XYZP Hospitality Group for the first half of the year (H1).

The objective was to analyze revenue performance across key business units, identify monthly trends, understand the drivers of revenue changes, and provide actionable insights to support management decision-making.

The project demonstrates the application of data analysis, financial reporting, data visualization, and business intelligence techniques.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Management needed a clear view of H1 revenue performance to answer key business questions:

- How much revenue was generated during H1?
- Which business unit contributed the most revenue?
- Which months performed best and worst?
- What caused the decline in revenue in June?
- How did room occupancy change throughout H1?
- Which revenue streams require management attention?
- Which business unit demonstrated the strongest resilience?

## 💡 Key Insights

### Revenue Trend

Revenue increased significantly from February into March and April, with April recording the strongest monthly performance.

Revenue subsequently declined in May and June.

### June Revenue Decline

June revenue declined by approximately **22.7%** compared with May.

The main contributors to the decline were:

- Room Sales: ↓ 34.1%
- Food Sales: ↓ 28.7%
- Sundry Sales: ↓ 38.3%

### Beverage Performance

Beverage Sales increased by approximately **11.2%** from May to June, making it the most resilient revenue stream during the period.

### Room Occupancy

Room occupancy increased from 28% in January to a H1 peak of 61% in April before declining to 32% in June.

---

## 🧠 Management Recommendations

Based on the analysis, management should:

1. Investigate the decline in room sales and identify factors affecting occupancy and room demand.

2. Review food sales performance, including pricing, menu performance, cost structure, and promotional strategies.

3. Investigate the significant decline in sundry sales and identify opportunities to improve ancillary revenue.

4. Maintain and strengthen beverage sales strategies given the positive performance observed in June.

5. Introduce targeted promotions during weaker months to improve demand and revenue performance.

6. Monitor room occupancy alongside room revenue to better understand the relationship between occupancy and room sales.
